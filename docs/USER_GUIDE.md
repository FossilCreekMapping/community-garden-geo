# Woodhaven Gardens — Map User Guide

Welcome to the mapping system for the **Woodhaven Living Laboratory**, managed
by [Econautics](https://econautics.org/). This guide is for garden coordinators,
educators, board members, and volunteers who use the maps day-to-day — no
technical background required.

The maps give everyone a shared, up-to-date picture of what is **on the land**
(beds, habitat, water, structures), what is **planned**, and where **education
and gathering** happen — supporting stewardship and community programs at the
Woodhaven hub.

---

## What you need

1. An **ArcGIS Online** account (your coordinator will invite you).
2. The **Field Maps** app on your phone or tablet:
   - [iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/arcgis-field-maps/id1471422156)
   - [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.esri.fieldmaps)
3. Optionally, a desktop web browser to view the same map at
   [arcgis.com](https://www.arcgis.com).

---

## Opening the maps

### On your phone or tablet (Field Maps)

1. Open **ArcGIS Field Maps**.
2. Sign in with your ArcGIS Online account.
3. Find **"Woodhaven Gardens - Operations"** in your maps list and tap to open.

If your coordinator shared a **QR code or link**, scan or tap it to go straight
to the map.

### On a desktop browser

1. Go to [arcgis.com](https://www.arcgis.com) and sign in.
2. Search your content or organization for **"Woodhaven Gardens - Operations"**.
3. Click the map to open it in Map Viewer.

## Understanding the layers

Each layer in the map represents a different type of information on the
property. Here is what each one is for and when you might edit it.

| Layer | What it represents | When to add or edit |
|---|---|---|
| **Property Boundary** | The overall property outline. | Rarely — only when a survey update occurs. Contact the GIS admin first. |
| **Management Zone** | Named areas with a purpose (vegetable production, pollinator, outdoor classroom, compost, etc.). | When a new zone is established or an existing one changes purpose or status. |
| **Garden Bed** | Individual planting beds or rows. | Assign a bed to a person, update its role (production, education, demo), or change its status at season transitions. |
| **Crop Season** | Seasonal crop records linked to a garden bed (crop name, variety, planting date, harvest, yield, pest issues). | At the start and end of each growing season. Add a new record per bed per season — this builds a history without changing the bed shape. Use **Program Notes** if the bed is part of a school or workshop activity. |
| **Planned Area** | Future development footprints (proposed pollinator patch, new greenhouse, etc.). | When the board approves a new project or its status changes (proposed → approved → in progress → completed). |
| **Gathering Area** | Picnic areas, outdoor classrooms, workshop spaces, fire rings. | When a new space is created or its capacity/policy changes. |
| **Trail** | Walking paths and interpretive loops. | When a new trail is built or surface/accessibility changes. |
| **Plant** | Individual plants and notable trees (species, health, source). | When a new plant is added, or to update health status during walk-throughs. |
| **Amenity** | Benches, picnic tables, signs, learning stations, water spigots, gates, tool lockers. | When new amenities are installed, moved, or need condition updates. |
| **Equipment** | Movable equipment (tillers, mowers, hose reels, storage bins). | When equipment is added, relocated, or needs condition updates. |
| **Building Footprint** | Sheds, greenhouses, pavilions, pump houses. | When a new structure is built or condition changes. |
| **Water Body** | Ponds, rain gardens, detention basins. | When a new water feature is created or notes need updating. |
| **Property Marker** | Survey pins, corner markers, wayfinding signs. | Rarely — after a new survey or when markers are placed. |

## Common workflows

### Start of a growing season

1. Open each **Garden Bed** you are responsible for.
2. Confirm the **assignee** and **status** (set to "Active").
3. Add a new **Crop Season** record: enter the crop, variety, and plant date.
   If this bed is part of a school program, note it in **Program Notes**.

### Mid-season check

1. Walk the garden with Field Maps open.
2. Tap a **Plant** to update its **health** status.
3. Tap an **Amenity** or **Equipment** to report condition changes.

### End of season

1. Open each bed's **Crop Season** record.
2. Fill in **harvest end date**, **yield notes**, and any **pest issues**.
3. Set the bed **status** to "Fallow" or "Available" if it will not be planted
   again immediately.

### Planning a new area

1. Draw a polygon on the **Planned Area** layer where the new feature will go.
2. Set the **status** to "Proposed" and fill in the description and priority.
3. When the board approves it, update the status to "Approved."
4. Once construction starts, change to "In Progress" and eventually "Completed."

## Field Maps tips

- **Syncing**: If you work offline, Field Maps will sync your edits when you
  reconnect to the internet. A blue dot on the sync icon means there are
  unsynced edits — tap it when you have a connection.
- **Photos**: If your coordinator has enabled attachments, you can tap the camera
  icon on any feature to attach a photo.
- **Editing permissions**: You may only be able to edit certain layers. If a
  layer appears grayed out, you have view-only access — contact your coordinator
  if you need edit rights.

## Boundaries and accuracy

Property boundary and corner markers may be **approximate** unless based on a
professional survey. Do not move or edit boundary features without checking with
the site coordinator or GIS admin first.

GPS accuracy on a phone is typically 3–5 meters. For beds and amenities this is
usually fine; for precise property corners, a survey-grade measurement is
preferred.
